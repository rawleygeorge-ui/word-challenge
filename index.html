    How to play

    Two players. One sets a secret word, the other rebuilds it on the board.

    Send a challenge

    1. Tap Send a challenge and enter your name and your opponent's name.
    2. Pick a difficulty. High is the default.
    3. Tap the die to roll. The game has secretly rolled its own die too: that's the hidden lucky number.
    4. If your roll matches the hidden number, it's a jackpot and you choose how many tiles you want, up to 20. Otherwise your roll decides: roll + 10 tiles on High (11–16), roll + 8 on Medium, roll + 6 on Easy.
    5. The letters on your tiles are always random, like a lucky draw.
    6. Tap tiles to spell your secret word. Use as many as you like, as long as the word is long enough for your level.
    7. Stuck with a bad hand? You get one free redraw.
    8. On Easy and Medium you can add a hint. Then tap Create challenge and send the link or 5-letter code on WhatsApp.

    Solve a challenge

    1. Open the link, or type the code on the home screen. You have 23 hours from when it was sent.
    2. Your scrambled tiles sit on the wooden rack. Tap a tile, then tap a square to place it. Tap a placed tile to take it back.
    3. Lay the word in one straight line through the centre flower.
    4. Tap Check word.

    Board rules

    • Words go across (read left to right) or down (read top to bottom). Nothing else.
    • No diagonals, no reading backwards, no gaps between tiles.
    • Breaking a board rule doesn't use up a try. Only a wrong word does.
    • Your rack holds every tile the setter was dealt. The ones they didn't use are decoys, so leave them on the rack.

    Levels

    Easy
    Roll + 6 tiles (7–12). Word of 3+ letters, 3 tries, hints allowed.
    Medium
    Roll + 8 tiles (9–14). Word of 5+ letters, 3 tries, points ×1.5.
    High
    Roll + 10 tiles (11–16). Word of 7+ letters, 2 tries, no hint, points ×2.
    Jackpot
    On any level, match the hidden lucky number and choose up to 20 tiles.

    Scoring

    • Every letter has a point value, shown in the corner of its tile.
    • Coloured squares boost your score: double or triple a letter, or double or triple the whole word. The centre flower doubles the word.
    • Place your word to cover the best squares, then the level bonus applies.
    • Solve it on a later try and you keep less: 60% then 30% (Easy, Medium), or 50% on High's second try.
    • Points go to the solver and add up on the Leaderboard.

    Good to know

    • Unanswered challenges expire after 23 hours and the word is revealed.
    • My challenges lists words waiting for you and words you've sent.
    • Add the game to your home screen: on Android tap Install when asked; on iPhone tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
